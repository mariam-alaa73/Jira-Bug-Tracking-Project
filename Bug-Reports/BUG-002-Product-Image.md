# BUG-002 — Product image is not displayed on product details page

## Bug Summary

The product image is not displayed on the product details page.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Products page.
2. Select any product.
3. Open the product details page.

## Expected Result

The selected product image should be displayed correctly.

## Actual Result

The product image is not displayed.

Medium

## Severity
