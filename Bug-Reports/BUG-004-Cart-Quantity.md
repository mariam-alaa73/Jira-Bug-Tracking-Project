# BUG-004 — Cart quantity is not updated after changing the product quantity

## Bug Summary

The product quantity is not updated in the cart after changing it.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Products page.
2. Add any product to the cart.
3. Open the Cart.
4. Change the product quantity.

## Expected Result

The cart should display the updated product quantity and total price.

## Actual Result

The product quantity remains unchanged after editing it.
